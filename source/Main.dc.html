Our core values

Solve Customer Problems

We start with the why—everything we do must solve real problems for our customers. If we're not addressing their pain points, we're not creating value, and without value, there's no path to growth.

Start Simple to Avoid Complexity and Prioritize Impact—The 80/20 Rule

Starting simple helps us avoid unnecessary complexity and ensures we can focus on what matters most. Complexity often creeps in when we overthink or overbuild, making progress harder. By starting simple, we can deliver progress over perfection—solving problems faster, prioritizing the most impactful actions, and refining as we go.

100% Alignment, 80% Accuracy

100% alignment means we're clear on the direction, goals, and immediate next steps as a team. It's about shared understanding, not perfect precision. Progress over perfection happens when we align fully on where we're going and take confident action, even if the details are only 80% accurate. Avoid getting stuck on unnecessary details—refine as you go to keep moving forward.

Be Direct—Don't Tiptoe Around Issues

We address challenges and give feedback directly, candidly, and constructively. Tiptoeing around issues or avoiding hard conversations slows us down and leads to bigger problems later. Being clear and direct allows us to align quickly, fix what's not working, and move forward together.

Own It and Support Each Other

Taking ownership means being accountable for results—not just tasks. At the same time, success happens as a team—we support each other, step up where needed, and share responsibility for achieving outcomes.

Build Human Connections

Trust, understanding, and connection are at the heart of how we collaborate. Strong relationships make work smoother, faster, and more rewarding. Without human connection, work feels transactional; with it, we thrive together.
